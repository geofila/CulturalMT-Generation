CulturalMT combines specialist-reviewed translation resources with connected heritage vocabularies to support precise terminology and traceable sources.

01 / HUMAN REVIEW

A specialist-reviewed foundation.

Our work with domain specialists is reflected in the CulturalMT reference collection: 341 final human-reviewed Greek-to-English translations in the public dataset’s test split.

A silver training set of approximately 80,000 automatically prepared translations complements the reviewed test collection. The reference records retain links to their original SearchCulture entries.
